A Hidden Gem

A Little Bit of Luck in Every Cup

WE LUCKY COFFEE is a spacious, peaceful café just off the busy Chinese Quarter streets — five minutes' walk from the Bullring.

Our four-leaf clover says it all: we want every guest to leave with a little luck. We pour carefully selected Arabica beans in a master-roasted golden blend, blend drinks with real fresh fruit, and keep the music low so you can study, work or catch up with friends in peace.
